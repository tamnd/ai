# Text checklist: hardware, v1

The text checklist of a hardware page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Architecture" name the design features that matter for AI workloads, as the vendor and independent sources describe them?
2. Are the vendor's performance numbers attributed to the vendor, with their precision, sparsity and other conditions?
3. Does node.md keep peak numbers apart from measured ones?
4. Does the note of "Use in AI" assign no training run to this hardware without a source?
5. Does the note of "Comparison" compare like with like (precision, form factor, generation)?
6. Are availability, price and export-control statements dated and sourced?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a vendor page or another source you know?
