# Text checklist: task, v1

The text checklist of a task page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the task is and why it matters, in our words?
2. Is "In plain words" right, and can a reader follow it with no notation?
3. Does "Definition" say exactly what the input is, what the output is, and what makes an output right?
4. Does "Definition" agree with contract.yaml on the input, the output and the scoring?
5. Does the note of "Metrics" say right which metric is used when, with sources?
6. Does the note of "Benchmarks" say right what each benchmark tests, and where it differs from the task as defined?
7. Does "Approaches" give the method families in historical order, with each sentence right?
8. Does "Difficulties" name the known ambiguities, annotation disagreements and shortcuts, with sources?
9. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
10. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
11. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
12. Does node.md leave every ranking of approaches to the computed Results table, with none in the site voice?
13. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
