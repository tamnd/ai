# Text checklist: architecture, v1

The text checklist of a architecture page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the architecture is and why it matters, in our words?
2. Is "In plain words" right, and can a reader follow it with no notation?
3. Does "Overview" describe the structure right, and does its figure or scene match "Components"?
4. Are the equations of each block in "Components" those of the original source, up to the notation map, in data-flow order?
5. Does the note of "Shapes and parameters" agree with shapes.yaml, and does the parameter-count derivation follow?
6. Are the FLOP and memory counts of "Computation and cost" derived right, including how they grow with the input size?
7. Does "Training" give only the recipes (objective, initialization, normalization placement) that its sources report?
8. Is each item of "Inductive biases" a right statement of what the design assumes about the data?
9. Does "Limitations" include the weaknesses that the canon and the main reviews name, each with a source?
10. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
11. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
12. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
13. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
14. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
