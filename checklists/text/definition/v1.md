# Text checklist: definition, v1

The text checklist of a definition page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Is "Definition" exact and the same as its cited source, in words and in the notation of the register?
2. Is the definition free of circularity: does it use no term that needs this term to be understood?
3. Are "Examples and non-examples" right, and does at least one non-example sit near the border?
4. Does "Usage" give the sense of each community that uses the term, each with a source, and miss no sense that the canon or a main glossary uses?
5. Does "Usage" report each sense without saying which one is right?
6. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
7. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
8. Where node.md says who coined the term or when, are the people, works and years right, and is any "first use" claim justified?
9. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
10. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
