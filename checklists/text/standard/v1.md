# Text checklist: standard, v1

The text checklist of a standard page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What it specifies" say right what the standard specifies, in our words?
2. Does "Structure" give the clause titles only, with no text of the clauses (19.3)?
3. Are the edition, the version and its date right?
4. Does the note of "Adoption and status" say nothing about adoption that a source does not report?
5. Does the note of "Relation to policy" say right which policies cite or implement the standard, per their official texts?
6. Does node.md claim no effect of conformity (safety, fairness, legality) that the standard or a source does not state?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the standard or another source you know?
