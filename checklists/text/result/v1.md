# Text checklist: result, v1

This kind has no page (06.3), so `aik review` has no text part for it. Samplers and reviewers use this list on the card.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the source, at its locator, give the score of the card?
2. Is the model the right release or checkpoint, not another member of its family?
3. Is the benchmark version right?
4. Does the setting (shots, prompt format, decoding, scoring) match what the source used?
5. Is self_reported true exactly when the source is the model's developer?
6. Is the date the date of the source, not of the harvest?
7. Is the result marked comparable only with results of the same version, setting and protocol (10.6)?
8. Does the summary state nothing that the card's fields do not support, with no rank and no "first"?
9. Is the summary in our words, not copied from the source?
