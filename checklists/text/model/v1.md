# Text checklist: model, v1

The text checklist of a model page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Overview" name the developer, the date and the role (family, release or checkpoint) right, with sources?
2. Does "Architecture" link the right base architecture and list every difference that the developer documents, and only those?
3. Does "Training data" report only what the developer documents, and say "undocumented" where the developer is silent?
4. Does "Training" take every number from facts.yaml, and name the estimator of every estimate?
5. Is every statement by the developer about its own model, capability claims included, attributed to the developer and marked self_reported: true in claims.yaml?
6. Does the note of "Results" say for each benchmark whether its scores are self-reported, third-party or measured here?
7. Does "Access and licence" give the licence and its main terms right, with an as_of date on each term that can change?
8. Does "Safety and evaluations" separate the developer's own evaluations from third-party ones, and name the access each evaluator had?
9. Does "Reception and impact" attribute every view to a named source, with supporters and critics given the same form?
10. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
11. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
12. Are the release dates of "Overview" and the history of "Access and licence" right as of their dates, and is every "first" claim justified?
13. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
14. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the model card, the technical report or another source you know?
