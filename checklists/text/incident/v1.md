# Text checklist: incident, v1

This kind is a card until it is promoted (06.3); use this list for the promoted page.
The text checklist of a incident page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What happened" give the date and the events as the sources report them, attributed, with no detail beyond them?
2. Do two independent reliable sources support the main account, or does node.md say that it rests on one?
3. Does "Systems and parties" name a private person only when 19.6 allows it (a public figure in the matter, two sources, needed to identify the incident)?
4. Does node.md name no minor, and describe the harm in no more detail than a reader needs to classify it?
5. Does "Harms" keep harms that sources report as having happened apart from harms alleged or feared?
6. Does "Response" attribute each statement of a party to that party?
7. Are the risk classes the right ones for what happened?
8. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
9. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
10. Does "What happened" name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
11. Does node.md assign no blame in the site voice?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
