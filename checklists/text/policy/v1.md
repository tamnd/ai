# Text checklist: policy, v1

The text checklist of a policy page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What it does" summarize the policy right, as the official text reads?
2. Does "Scope and definitions" say who and what the policy covers, with its key definitions in our words and the right article numbers?
3. Is the jurisdiction right, and does node.md say where the policy does not apply?
4. Do the group notes of "Main provisions" match the official text of the provisions?
5. Does "Obligations by actor" assign each obligation to the right actor (provider, deployer, importer, user, authority), per the official text?
6. Do the dates and the status of "Timeline" agree with the official text and every act that amends it?
7. Does "Enforcement and penalties" give the penalties and the enforcing bodies right, per the official text?
8. In "Interpretation and debate", does official guidance come first, and are the positions in the same form, with no side taken?
9. Is the official text the first reference, in the version in force at the as_of date?
10. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
11. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
12. Does node.md name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
13. Does node.md tell no reader what they must do, as legal advice would, and leave every view of the policy to a named source?
14. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the official text or a law firm's summary you know?
