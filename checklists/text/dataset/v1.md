# Text checklist: dataset, v1

The text checklist of a dataset page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Contents" say right what is in the dataset, by modality and by source?
2. Does "Collection and construction" describe the crawling, filtering, annotation or synthesis as the dataset paper or datasheet does, and name who did the work where it is documented?
3. Does "Documentation and licence" give the licence, the terms and the consent and opt-out mechanisms right, with an as_of date on each term that can change?
4. Is what the makers do not document said to be undocumented, rather than guessed?
5. Does "Known issues" include the personal data, harmful content, copyright, takedown and bias reports that sources make, each attributed?
6. Does node.md name no private person found in the data and quote no personal or harmful content from it?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
