# Text checklist: system, v1

The text checklist of a system page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Overview" say right what the system was or is, who built it, when and in which domain?
2. Does "How it works" describe its parts (models, search, rules, hardware) as the builders' papers do?
3. Does "Development" give the timeline right, with sources?
4. Is each item of "Results and milestones" dated, sourced and stated with its conditions (opponents, time controls, settings)?
5. Are the builders' statements about their own system attributed to them?
6. Does "Impact and reception" attribute every view to a named source?
7. Does "Limitations and incidents" include the documented failures and incidents, with sources?
8. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
9. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
10. Do "Development" and "Results and milestones" name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
11. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
