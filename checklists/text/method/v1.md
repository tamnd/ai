# Text checklist: method, v1

The text checklist of a method page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the method is and what it is for, in our words?
2. Is "In plain words" right, and can a reader follow it with no notation?
3. Does "Setting" state the problem, the assumptions, and the models and tasks the method applies to?
4. Are the equations of "The method" those of the original source, up to the notation map, and is the procedure complete?
5. Does every step of "Derivation" and of derivations/ follow, and does the numeric check test the stated result?
6. Is the "Worked example" right by hand?
7. Are the ranges and framework defaults of "Hyperparameters" right for the pinned versions, each with a source?
8. Does "Cost" give the extra compute and memory at training and at inference right, with the variables named?
9. Do "Limitations and failure modes", "Variants" and "Pitfalls" include those of the canon and of the main review articles?
10. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
11. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
12. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
13. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say; does nothing rank or date itself?
14. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
