# Text checklist: person, v1

The text checklist of a person page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead give the name as the person publishes it, their roles, and years only where a reliable source publishes them?
2. For a living person, or one who died less than two years ago, does node.md give no full date of birth?
3. Does node.md hold only public professional facts: no contact details, home, family, health, religion, private life or wealth (19.6)?
4. Does the note of "Contributions" credit the person with what sources credit them with, and no more, with co-authors named?
5. Does "Career" list public roles only, with the right years, each with a source?
6. Are the awards and years of "Recognition" right?
7. Is any criticism of the person about published work or public acts, from reliable sources, attributed and in neutral words?
8. Are the person's predictions and estimates given with source and date, never judged by the site?
9. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
10. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
11. Does node.md name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
12. Does node.md use no word of praise or blame ("pioneer", "visionary") in the site voice?
13. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a biography, an encyclopedia or another source you know?
