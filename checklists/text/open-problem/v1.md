# Text checklist: open-problem, v1

The text checklist of a open-problem page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "The question" state one exact question with its terms defined, and give each definition where the parties disagree?
2. Does "Why it matters" attribute what depends on the answer to named sources?
3. Does "Positions" include every serious position of the literature (07.7), and no position without the sources for it?
4. Does each position have the same parts in the same order: the claim, the strongest argument, the evidence and the objections?
5. Would a proponent of each position accept its statement?
6. Is the evidence shared: does each position link the claims it relies on, with no claim that bears on several positions shown under one only?
7. Does "What would settle it" give tests that the positions agree on, with sources, or say that they do not agree?
8. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
9. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
10. Does "History of the debate" name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
11. Does node.md take no side, in the order, the space or the words given to each position?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
