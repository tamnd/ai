# Text checklist, v1

The fallback for a kind with no list of its own under checklists/text/. The text part is node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the item is and why it matters, in our words?
2. Is "In plain words", where the kind has it, right, and can a reader follow it with no notation?
3. Does the main section of the kind (06.3) say what its sources say, with nothing left out that the canon treats as central?
4. Is every equation, procedure or definition the same as its cited source, up to the notation map?
5. Does every derivation step follow, and is the worked example, if any, right by hand?
6. Does every bold section of the kind hold what 06.3 asks of it, not only a heading and filler?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does every view on a contested point have a named holder and a source, with no side taken?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
