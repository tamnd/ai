# Text checklist: provision, v1

This kind is a card until it is promoted (06.3); use this list for the promoted page.
The text checklist of a provision page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What it requires" state in our words what the official text of the provision requires, adding and dropping nothing that changes it?
2. Does node.md cite the right article, paragraph and point, in the version in force at the as_of date?
3. Does "Applies to" name the right actors and systems, with the exceptions of the text?
4. Are the dates in "Dates" those of this provision, where they differ from the policy's entry into force?
5. Does "Official guidance" cite official guidance only, dated, kept apart from commentary?
6. Do "Related provisions" include the provisions that the text itself cross-references?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does node.md name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md tell no reader what they must do, as legal advice would?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the official text you know?
