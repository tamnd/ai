# Text checklist: organization, v1

The text checklist of a organization page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Overview" say right what the organization is (lab, company, regulator, funder ...), where it is, and its role in AI?
2. Does "History" give the founding, mergers and renamings with the right dates and sources?
3. Does the note of "Work" credit models, systems and works to this organization as sources do, not to a parent or a former name in error?
4. Does "People" list public roles only, with the right years, and no private facts (19.6)?
5. Are the organization's statements about itself (mission, safety, capability) attributed to it?
6. Are funding, ownership and size figures dated and from reliable sources, or left out?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the organization's own pages or another source you know?
