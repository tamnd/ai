# Text checklist: benchmark, v1

The text checklist of a benchmark page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What it measures" attribute the abilities to the benchmark's authors, and say what the benchmark does not measure?
2. Is "Data" right about how the data was built, where it came from and how it is split, per the benchmark paper?
3. Does "Protocol" give the shots, prompt format, decoding, scoring and answer extraction of each version, and say how the settings change scores?
4. Do the notes of "Versions" and "Results" keep scores of different versions and settings apart?
5. Does "Known issues" include the label errors, contamination reports, shortcuts and saturation that sources report, each with a source?
6. Does "Reception" attribute every view to a named source?
7. Does node.md reproduce no test item beyond what the licence allows and what a reader needs, so that it does not leak the test set?
8. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
9. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
10. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
11. Does node.md rank no model in the site voice, leaving comparisons to the computed tables?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
