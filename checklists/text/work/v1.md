# Text checklist: work, v1

This kind is a card until it is promoted (06.3); use this list for the promoted page.
The text checklist of a work page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Is each item of "Contributions" what the work introduced or showed, in our words, with a locator in the work?
2. Does "Contributions" credit the work with nothing that earlier work introduced?
3. Does "Context" name the problem and the prior work that the work answered, right?
4. Are the results of "Methods and results" stated within the scope of the work's experiments, and linked to result cards where they exist?
5. Does the note of "Criticism, replications and corrections" cover the errata, retractions and failed replications that sources report?
6. Does the note of "Reception and influence" attribute influence to sources, not to the writer's view?
7. Are the authors, venue, year and version right against the work itself?
8. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
9. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
10. Does node.md name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
11. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
12. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from the abstract or another source you know?
