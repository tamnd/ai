# Text checklist: area, v1

The text checklist of a area page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the area covers and why it matters, in our words?
2. Does Scope draw the border with the neighbour areas as the area tree and the canon textbooks draw it?
3. Does Map put each sub-area or node after its prerequisites, in an order a learner could follow?
4. Are the Key ideas the ideas that the canon textbooks of the area teach first, and is each bullet right?
5. Is no subtopic that the canon tables of contents give the area missing from Scope and Key ideas?
6. Does Scope report each school or approach of the area without presenting one as the right one?
7. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
8. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
9. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
10. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
11. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
