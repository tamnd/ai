# Text checklist: finding, v1

The text checklist of a finding page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the lead say what the finding is and why it matters, in our words?
2. Is "In plain words" right, and can a reader follow it with no notation?
3. Does "The finding" state the result in one precise sentence with its scope, and give its measured form with its source?
4. Does the note of "Evidence" cover the first report, the replications and the failures to replicate, each with the right role in claims.yaml?
5. Does "Scope and conditions" say where the finding holds, where it fails, and how measurement choices change it?
6. Are the competing explanations of "Explanations" each given in the same form, with sources, and none favoured?
7. Is each item of "Implications" attributed to a named source?
8. Does node.md describe each experiment of the node at its own scale, never as a reproduction of the paper's result?
9. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
10. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
11. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
12. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
13. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
