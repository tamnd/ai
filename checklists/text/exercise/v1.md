# Text checklist: exercise, v1

This kind has no page (06.3), so `aik review` has no text part for it. Samplers and reviewers use this list on the card.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the link open the exercise that the card names, on the public site?
2. Are the site, the course, the author and the year right?
3. Does the card hold no problem statement of its site, only the link, the metadata and a hint (19.3)?
4. Is the hint right, and does it give no full solution?
5. Are the linked nodes the ones that the exercise practises?
6. Is the level right for the prerequisites that the exercise needs?
7. Does the summary state nothing that the card's fields do not support?
8. Is the summary and the hint in our words, not copied from the site?
