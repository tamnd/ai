# Text checklist: theorem, v1

The text checklist of a theorem page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "Statement" give the exact result with all its hypotheses, the same as the cited source up to the notation map?
2. Is "In plain words" right, and does it drop no hypothesis that changes the meaning?
3. Does every step of "Proof" (or derivations/proof.md) follow, naming the definition, assumption or lemma it uses?
4. Does the proof cover every case, and track its constants or say where they are absorbed?
5. Does "Assumptions" say right what each hypothesis does, with a correct example of what fails without it?
6. Does "Tightness" give a correct example that the bound cannot improve, or say with a source that the tight form is open?
7. Does node.md claim no more than the statement proves, in domain, quantifiers or constants?
8. Does the Illustration, if any, show the bound against measured values without overstating it?
9. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
10. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
11. Does History name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
12. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
13. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a textbook or another source you know?
