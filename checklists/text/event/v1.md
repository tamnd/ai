# Text checklist: event, v1

This kind is a card until it is promoted (06.3); use this list for the promoted page.
The text checklist of a event page: node.md, claims.yaml and derivations/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does "What happened" give the date, the place and the participants right, with sources?
2. Does "What happened" keep the record of the time apart from later retellings?
3. Are the results of the event (scores, games, decisions, documents) right against the primary record?
4. Are the participants named for public roles only (19.6)?
5. Is each item of "Outcomes and significance" attributed to a named source?
6. Does every claim in claims.yaml have the right type, with a scope (empirical) or assumptions (mathematical) no wider than its sources, and does no sentence of node.md state more than its claim?
7. Pick three claims of claims.yaml at random: does the cited source, at its locator, say what each claim says, within its scope?
8. Does "What happened" name the right people, works and years, and is every "first" claim justified by its sources and clear about the sense of "first"?
9. Does node.md leave every judgement, rank and prediction to a named source or a computed fact, with nothing in the site voice that only a source should say?
10. Does no passage of node.md read as copied or closely paraphrased from a source you know?
