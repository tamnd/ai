# Lean statement checklist, v1

For the Lean module of a theorem node (F1, 11.7): the elaborated type of the cited theorem against the page's Statement.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the elaborated Lean statement say what the page's "Statement" says?
2. Do the hypotheses match the page's, and where Lean's are stronger, does the page say so?
3. Is the domain right (finite or general hypothesis classes, discounted or average reward, finite or infinite sets)?
4. Are the quantifiers right and in the right order (for all distributions, for some sample size)?
5. Are the constants and the form of the bound (a log(1/delta), the exact constant) the ones the page prints?
6. Do the definitions that the statement uses mean what the page's terms mean (coercions, truncated subtraction, division by zero)?
7. Is the statement not vacuous: does the module's example meet every hypothesis in a case that is not degenerate?
8. Where the proof marks steps (`-- step: <id>`), does each marked `have` state the page step it names?
9. Is every back-translation disagreement resolved or explained?
10. Does the page claim no more than Lean proves?
