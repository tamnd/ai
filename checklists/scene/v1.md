# Scene checklist, v1

For viz/ of a node (V3): the scene, its presets, its charts and its filmstrip, for clarity and accessibility. The build already runs the V rules of 12.14.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the scene show the objects that the page's derivation talks about, in the page's notation?
2. Does each phase's note say right what the step does, linked to the right equation or derivation step?
3. Can a reader who knows the prerequisites follow the default preset without the page text?
4. Do the presets show the cases worth seeing: the worked example, an edge case, a failure?
5. Are the colour scales readable, and does each state have a mark as well as a colour?
6. Does the text view give each step, with tensors as tables with headers?
7. Can the scene be used with the keyboard only, with visible focus and no focus trap?
8. With reduced motion, is the scene still usable, and does nothing flash more than three times a second?
9. Does the filmstrip, with no script, tell the same story?
10. For a chart: are the population rule and the "Data as of" date right, and does the chart imply no rank its data does not show?
11. Does nothing in the scene imply more than the page claims, such as a toy training run shown as the paper's result?
