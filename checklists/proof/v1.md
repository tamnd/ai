# Proof checklist, v1

For one proof or derivation of a node, `proof-<id>` (H3): derivations/<id>.md, checked against node.md.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does the "Statement" of the proof match the result that node.md states, or the claims it derives?
2. Are the assumptions numbered, each one used, and each one in the page's statement or the claim's assumes?
3. Does every step follow from the definition, assumption, lemma or algebraic rule it names?
4. Is every case covered, and does each induction name its hypothesis?
5. Does each inequality name the rule it uses, and are the constants tracked or stated as absorbed?
6. Is each cited lemma stated right and used within its hypotheses?
7. Is the status right: full only when no step is left out, sketch otherwise, with what it leaves out stated?
8. Does the numeric check, if any, test the stated result, at inputs that would catch an error?
9. Does "Result and check" restate the result as "Statement" gives it?
10. Are the claims in `derives` the ones this proof supports, and no more?
11. Is the proof written in our words, with any proof taken from a source cited, not pasted?
