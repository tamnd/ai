# Facts checklist: model, v1

For facts.yaml of a model node. Most model facts are self-reported, and many change; check each against the developer's own documents and the date.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Does each parameter fact (total, and active for a mixture of experts) match the developer's paper or model card at its locator?
2. Does every value describe this release or checkpoint, not another member of the family?
3. Are the developer's paper, model card and blog counted as one source, never as independent sources that agree?
4. Where the developer does not disclose a fact (compute, tokens, data), is there no value but a no_primary note with the date of the check?
5. Is each estimate (Epoch AI or another) attributed to its estimator with its method, never given as the developer's value?
6. Are the release date and the dates of the versions right, per the developer's announcement?
7. Is the licence that of the weights (not of the code), and is access right as of its as_of?
8. Are the context length, the modalities and the architecture right for this checkpoint?
9. Are training compute and tokens in the right units (FLOP, tokens), with no more precision than the source?
10. Is no fact taken from a leak, a rumour or a social media post?
11. Is each as_of the date when the source said the value, not the date of the harvest?
12. Pick three facts at random: does a source you find yourself agree, or is the difference recorded?
