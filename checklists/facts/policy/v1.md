# Facts checklist: policy, v1

For facts.yaml of a policy node. The reviewer needs `law:<jurisdiction>` (15.5).
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Are the jurisdiction and the type (law, regulation, treaty, voluntary framework, lab policy) right?
2. Is the official text link the authentic version (for EU law, EUR-Lex with its CELEX number)?
3. Do the dates of adoption, publication, entry into force and application of each part match the official text, including every act that amends them?
4. Does each date name the part of the policy that it applies to?
5. Is the status right as of its as_of?
6. Is each fact sourced to the official text, the official journal or the body that made the policy, where one exists?
7. Where a law firm, a news report or another secondary source differs from the official text, is the official value the primary one and the other recorded?
8. Are the article numbers right for the version cited (consolidated or as adopted)?
9. Is no fact taken from an unofficial translation where an official language version exists?
10. Is each as_of the date of the check against the official text?
