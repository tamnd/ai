# Facts checklist, v1

The fallback for facts.yaml of a kind with no list of its own under checklists/facts/.
Answer each question pass, fail or n/a; give a note for each fail. Yes means pass.

1. Pick five facts at random (all, if fewer): does each primary source, at its locator, give the value and the unit of facts.yaml?
2. Is each source marked primary really primary (the maker's paper, card or documentation, or the official text), and each secondary one really secondary?
3. Where a primary source exists, is it cited, rather than only a secondary one?
4. Is each no_primary reason true: did you find no primary source that gives the value?
5. Is each estimate attributed to its estimator with its method, not given as a reported value?
6. Where two sources differ, are both values recorded, so that the build can mark the fact disputed?
7. Is the as_of of each time-bound fact the date when its source said it?
8. Is the meaning of each field right (total or active, items or tokens, peak or sustained), with the right unit and no more precision than the source?
9. Does every value describe this item (this version, release or checkpoint), not a sibling?
10. Are the required facts of the kind (schema/facts.v1.yaml) present, or their absence explained?
11. Does each locator point at the place in the source that holds the value (a table, a section, a page)?
